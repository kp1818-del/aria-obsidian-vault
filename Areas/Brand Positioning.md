## Brand Narratives

### Praxis.ai
*"AI that helps people run the business better."*
- Bias: AI-native operating layer
- Best for: Scalable growth, modern teams, long-term platform vision
- Footer: *Practical intelligence for modern business.*

### Praxis.co
*"Where growth, operations, and people align."*
- Bias: Executive-safe, premium, broadly applicable
- Best for: Conservative buyers, multi-location businesses, enterprise onboarding
- Footer: *Infrastructure for how work gets done.*

### Axiom.ai
*"Business performance, governed by principle."*
- Bias: Governance, institutional trust
- Best for: Regulated industries, board-level conversations
- Footer: *Performance with principles.*

### Axiom.co
*"The standard for sustainable business performance."*
- Bias: Calm authority, gold-standard
- Best for: Enterprises, boards, conservative leadership
- Footer: *The infrastructure behind trusted organizations.*

---

## Recommendation
**Primary pick: Praxis.ai** — cleanly supports all four pillars without sounding bloated.
**Defensive registrations:** Praxis.co · Praxis.io · PraxisHQ.com
