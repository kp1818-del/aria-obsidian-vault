# AI Business Summit — Key Prompts

> From Alicia Lyttle's AI Business Summit. Reusable prompts for business + content.

---

## Business Ideation Prompt
*"You have $500 and one weekend to help me. Based on what you know about me, give me 10 ways I can make money using AI this weekend. For each idea: make it specific to my background, my audience, my existing assets, and my brand. One idea should incorporate AI Agents, another AI Consulting. Write a 3-4 sentence description, then 'Getting Started in 3 Steps' — Step 1: proof of concept, Step 2: promote to existing audience, Step 3: exactly where to find the client and what to say."*

---

## Prompt 1 — Roast My Bio
*"Here is my current bio: [paste bio]. Please rewrite it three ways: one powerful and professional, one warm and relatable, one bold and exciting. Then tell me which is strongest and why."*

## Prompt 2 — Pitch Perfect
*"I sell [product/service] to [ideal customer]. Write me a 30-second elevator pitch, a LinkedIn post I can publish today, and three cold email subject lines that would make someone open my email."*

## Prompt 3 — Make Me Famous (in advance)
*"Based on what you know about me, write: a press release announcing I won a major industry award, a Wikipedia-style bio, and a book jacket blurb for a book I wrote about my expertise."*

## Prompt 4 — Fix My Email
*"Here is an email I need to send: [paste email]. Rewrite it three ways: professional and polished, warm and friendly, bold and confident. Then write five subject line options."*

## Prompt 5 — Sales Pitch Roleplay
*"I want to practice my sales pitch. Play a skeptical potential customer with three objections: too expensive, no time to learn, tried something similar before and it didn't work. Push back on everything. I'll go first."*

## AI Agent Chief of Staff Prompt
```
You are [AGENT NAME], my AI Chief of Staff.
I am [YOUR NAME], [YOUR TITLE].
My email is [YOUR EMAIL]. I use Telegram.

Every morning at [TIME], send me a briefing with:
1. My top 3 priorities for the day
2. What's on my calendar today
3. Any emails that need a reply — draft the replies for me

Check my email every 30 minutes. Alert me instantly if anything urgent arrives.
When I ask you to send an email, draft it in my voice and confirm before sending.

Let's go. What do you need from me to get started?
```

## PWA App Builder Prompt (Manus)
*"Act as a Business Innovation Consultant. Ask me 10 questions, one at a time, about myself, my business, my audience, my skills, and my goals. Based on my answers, suggest 5 simple PWA ideas I can build with Manus. Each idea: description, who it's for, why valuable, buildable in one session. After suggesting ideas, ask which excites me most and help me write the perfect prompt to build it."*

---

## Daily AI News Prompt
*"Every day at 5am send me the latest AI news including OpenAI, Meta, Claude, and at least 7 updates. Turn those updates into a TikTok script, a LinkedIn post, and a blog post."*
