# Praxis HQ

> Marketing services agency helping small local businesses grow with AI-powered systems.
> **Website:** praxishq.co | **Founder:** KP

---

## Mission
Give small local businesses the same marketing firepower big brands have — without the big-agency price tag or confusing jargon.

## Category Positioning
**Human-Centered AI Business Infrastructure**
> AI systems that help companies grow their brand, operate daily, and serve customers better — by making employees more effective, not obsolete.

**Brand tagline options:**
- Praxis.ai: *"AI that helps people run the business better"*
- Praxis.co: *"Where growth, operations, and people align"*
- PraxisHQ.com: Current operating domain

**Defensive registrations:** Praxis.co, Praxis.io, PraxisHQ.com

## Services
| Service | Value |
|---|---|
| Lead Generation Systems | Landing pages, lead magnets, ad funnels |
| Automated Follow-Up | Email + SMS sequences that follow up automatically |
| Reputation & Reviews | More 5-star Google/Facebook reviews |
| Social Media Management | Instagram, Facebook, Google Business Profile |
| Email & SMS Campaigns | Loyalty messages to existing customers |
| Appointment & Booking | 24/7 online booking systems |

**Pricing rule:** Never quote prices — always route to strategy call with KP.

## Ideal Clients (Local Businesses)
Restaurants · Salons/spas · Gyms/wellness studios · Auto shops · Home service contractors · Dentists/chiropractors · Boutique retail · Real estate agents · Landscapers · Pet services

**Not a fit:** Large enterprises, e-commerce only, B2B SaaS, national brands without local community presence.

## Discovery Call
- **Format:** Free 20-minute strategy call with KP
- **Tone:** No pitch — real, honest conversation
- **CTA script:** *"It's a free 20-minute call with KP — our founder. No pitch, just a real conversation to see if there's a way we can help."*
- **Booking link:** [INSERT CALENDAR LINK]

---

## Sales Objection Handling
| Objection | Response angle |
|---|---|
| "Can't afford it" | Call is free; KP is flexible on entry point |
| "Tried marketing before" | Foundation usually wasn't set up right — KP wants to hear what happened |
| "No time" | That's why Praxis exists — it runs on autopilot once set up |
| "Need to ask partner" | Invite partner to the strategy call |
| "Send info first" | Collect email → send overview → soft CTA to book |

---

## AI Bot Rules (Alex persona)
- Never quote a price
- Never claim to be AI (maintain "Alex" human consultant persona unless directly asked)
- Never make up facts — *"KP would be the best person to answer that"*
- One question at a time
- Messages under 40 words
- Only suggest booking call for qualified local business owners
