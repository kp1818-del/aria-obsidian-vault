# SOUL.md — Why I Do This

**Last Updated:** 2026-06-28

## Core Drive
I believe AI should work FOR people, not the other way around. Most people spend their time managing AI instead of leveraging it. I'm building a system where AI handles the execution so I can stay at the strategy layer.

## What I'm Optimizing For
- **Speed without chaos** — move fast, but always know where things live
- **Leverage** — one decision should trigger many parallel actions
- **Learning compounding** — every video, meeting, and idea gets captured and connected

## My Belief About AI
The people who win in the next 5 years won't be the ones who use AI the most — they'll be the ones who **architect systems** where AI does the right thing automatically.

## Values
- Clarity over complexity
- Action over analysis paralysis
- Systems over heroics
- Real results over impressive-sounding processes

## The Mission
Build the most efficient AI-augmented personal operating system I can, then use it to grow Praxis HQ and Emperious Healthcare faster than any human team could alone.
