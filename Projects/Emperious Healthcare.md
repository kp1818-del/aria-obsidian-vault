# Emperious Healthcare

> Healthcare venture focused on home health and adult day care services in Pennsylvania.
> Targeting Medicaid-heavy populations in the Harrisburg / Lancaster / York corridor.

---

## Market Focus
- **Geography:** 20-mile radius around Harrisburg, Lancaster, and York, PA
- **Counties:** Dauphin, Lancaster, York
- **Primary goal:** Identify best county + service line for rapid Medicaid-heavy ramp-up
- **Payer strategy:** Medicaid-heavy (Pennsylvania CHC / LTSS waiver programs)

---

## Service Lines Under Evaluation
1. **Home Health** — skilled nursing, therapy, in-home care
2. **Adult Day Care** — community-based day programs for elderly/disabled

**Decision needed:** Choose first service line AND first launch county, then define fastest path to compliant enrollment, referral flow, and first paid starts.

---

## Market Entry Scoring Model
Weight these factors per county:
- Medicaid concentration
- Referral density (hospitals, AAAs, discharge planners)
- Reimbursement attractiveness (PA fee schedules, CHC rates)
- Licensing complexity
- Staffing + transportation burden
- Speed-to-launch score

---

## Key Referral Sources
- Hospitals and discharge planners
- Area Agencies on Aging (AAAs)
- County Assistance Offices
- High-referral community partners

---
